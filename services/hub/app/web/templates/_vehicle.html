{# Vehicle render — the core visual asset of the product (§11). Tesla's own studio render, background removed with a segmentation model and given a contact shadow so the vehicle sits on the page rather than floating on it. The drawn SVG stays as the fallback: Tesla's configurator compositor serves S/3/X/Y but returns 412 for Cybertruck, so both paths have to exist. State reads as light on the ground the vehicle stands on, not as marks on the body. On a photograph we cannot know where this car's charge port or lamps actually are, and guessing would put a glowing dot on a door panel. #}
